BEAVER'S CHOICE PAPER COMPANY — MULTI-AGENT SYSTEM REFLECTIVE REPORT & DESIGN NOTES
====================================================================================

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
--------------------
This document details the architecture, implementation, evaluation results, and future
improvement recommendations for the Beaver's Choice Paper Company Multi-Agent System,
implemented in `project_starter.py` using the `smolagents` framework (`ToolCallingAgent`
and `OpenAIServerModel`) and backed by an SQLite database (`beaver.db`).

The system meets all project requirements:
- Uses at most 5 agents (implemented with 4 agents: 1 Orchestrator + 3 Worker Agents).
- Integrates all 7 starter helper functions inside `smolagents` `@tool` definitions.
- Processes all 20 customer scenarios in `quote_requests_sample.csv` and logs outputs
  to `test_results.csv`.
- Fulfilled 17 orders (5 full, 12 partial) and actively rejected 3 full orders (#13, #15, #18)
  plus item-level rejections when stock/supplier delivery deadlines or catalog constraints
  could not be satisfied.


2. COMPREHENSIVE EXPLANATION OF THE MULTI-AGENT SYSTEM
------------------------------------------------------

2.1 Architecture & Workflow Overview (See `workflow_diagram.png`)
The system uses a centralized hub-and-spoke orchestration architecture:
1. Customer Inquiry Input: The `OrchestratorAgent` receives the natural-language customer
   inquiry, request date (`as_of_date`), and requested delivery deadline.
2. Step 1 - Inventory & Delivery Verification (`InventoryAgent`):
   The Orchestrator delegates inventory verification to the `InventoryAgent`, which maps
   informal item descriptions to exact catalog names in `paper_supplies`, checks current
   warehouse stock as of the request date, and checks supplier restock delivery dates for
   any shortfall.
3. Step 2 - Quote Generation & Strategic Bulk Discounting (`QuotingAgent`):
   The Orchestrator passes the verified inventory report to the `QuotingAgent`, which
   queries historical quotes for similar customer requests and computes itemized pricing
   with tiered bulk discounts (5% for 100-499 units, 10% for 500-999 units, 15% for 1,000+
   units).
4. Step 3 - Order Fulfillment & Financial Reporting (`TransactionAgent`):
   For all fulfillable items whose delivery dates meet the customer's deadline, the
   Orchestrator instructs the `TransactionAgent` to verify company cash balance, record
   supplier restock orders (`stock_orders`) if needed, record customer sales (`sales`),
   and generate an updated financial report.
5. Step 4 - Final Customer Response (`OrchestratorAgent`):
   The Orchestrator synthesizes the outputs from all three worker agents into a clear,
   polite customer response with itemized pricing, bulk discounts, delivery dates, and
   explicit reasons for any unfulfilled items.

2.2 Agent Roles & Tool Mapping (All 7 Starter Helper Functions Utilized)
- `OrchestratorAgent`:
  Central coordinator that manages task delegation across the three specialized worker
  agents (`InventoryAgent`, `QuotingAgent`, `TransactionAgent`) and compiles the final
  customer response.

- `InventoryAgent`:
  Tools:
  * `check_all_inventory(as_of_date)` -> calls `get_all_inventory(as_of_date)`
  * `check_item_stock(item_name, quantity_needed, as_of_date, delivery_deadline)`
    -> calls `get_stock_level(resolved_name, as_of_date)` and
       `get_supplier_delivery_date(as_of_date, recommended_reorder_qty)`
  * `check_supplier_delivery(input_date_str, quantity)`
    -> calls `get_supplier_delivery_date(input_date_str, quantity)`

- `QuotingAgent`:
  Tools:
  * `get_quote_history(search_terms, limit)` -> calls `search_quote_history(search_terms, limit)`
  * `calculate_bulk_quote(item_name, quantity, as_of_date)` -> looks up base `unit_price`
    in `paper_supplies` and applies tiered bulk discounts (5% / 10% / 15%).

- `TransactionAgent`:
  Tools:
  * `check_company_cash(as_of_date)` -> calls `get_cash_balance(as_of_date)`
  * `record_order_transaction(item_name, transaction_type, quantity, price, date, delivery_deadline)`
    -> calls `get_stock_level`, `get_supplier_delivery_date`, `get_cash_balance`, and
       `create_transaction` (for both `'stock_orders'` and `'sales'`)
  * `get_company_financial_report(as_of_date)` -> calls `generate_financial_report(as_of_date)`


3. EVALUATION RESULTS (`test_results.csv`)
------------------------------------------

3.1 Quantitative Summary Across 20 Test Scenarios (2025-04-01 to 2025-04-17)
- Initial Cash Balance (2025-04-01):      $45,059.70
- Final Cash Balance (2025-04-17):        $45,210.65  (Net Cash Gain: +$150.95)
- Initial Inventory Value (2025-04-01):   $4,940.30
- Final Inventory Value (2025-04-17):     $4,263.90
- Total Customer Requests Evaluated:      20
- Orders Fulfilled (Full or Partial):     17 orders (exceeds minimum requirement of >= 3)
  * Full Fulfillments (5): Requests #6, #7, #8, #10, #12
  * Partial Fulfillments (12): Requests #1, #2, #3, #4, #5, #9, #11, #14, #16, #17, #19, #20
- Orders Completely Rejected:             3 orders (exceeds minimum requirement of >= 1)
  * Request #13 (2025-04-08, deadline 2025-04-10): Rejected 500 A4 paper and 200 Cardstock
    because warehouse stock was insufficient and supplier restock arrived on 2025-04-12,
    after the April 10 deadline.
  * Request #15 (2025-04-12, deadline 2025-04-15): Rejected 10,000 A4 paper (arrives
    2025-04-19), 5,000 Colored paper (arrives 2025-04-19), and 500 Heavyweight paper
    (arrives 2025-04-16) because supplier restock lead time exceeded the April 15 deadline.
  * Request #18 (2025-04-14, deadline 2025-04-15): Rejected 500 Cardstock, 1,000 Standard
    copy paper, and 200 Colored paper because supplier restock arrived on 2025-04-18,
    after the next-day April 15 deadline.

3.2 Strengths Observed
1. Accurate Constraint Enforcement:
   Every order item was validated against current stock and `get_supplier_delivery_date`.
   Whenever supplier delivery exceeded the customer's requested date, the item or order
   was actively rejected with specific stock counts and delivery dates explained to the
   customer.
2. Partial Order Fulfillment:
   For multi-item inquiries where some items were out of catalog (e.g., "balloons" in
   Request #2, "A3 paper" in Request #3, "tickets" in Request #20) or had insufficient
   stock / missed delivery deadlines, the system fulfilled available items while clearly
   explaining the unfulfilled ones.
3. Robust Synonym Resolution:
   `resolve_catalog_item` accurately mapped informal customer terms ("party streamers" ->
   "Party streamers", "washi tape" -> "Decorative adhesive tape (washi tape)", "poster board"
   -> "Large poster paper (24x36 inches)") to canonical database names.

3.3 Areas for Improvement Observed
1. Wholesale vs. Retail Unit Pricing on Restocked Items:
   Because `paper_supplies` uses a single `unit_price` for both supplier restock cost
   (`stock_orders`) and base customer retail price (`sales`), restocking an out-of-stock
   item at 100% of `unit_price` and selling it with a 5%-15% bulk discount yields a slight
   negative margin on the restocked portion.
2. Packaging Unit Normalization:
   In Request #3 ("500 reams of printer paper"), the quantity 500 was priced at the
   per-sheet rate ($0.04) rather than converting reams to sheets (e.g., 500 sheets/ream).
3. LLM Interpretation of Restock Eligibility When `current_stock == 0`:
   In a few scenarios with a longer delivery window (e.g., Request #5 for 200 Washi tape
   and Request #11 for 300 Matte paper), even though `check_item_stock` returned
   `can_fulfill: True` via supplier restock, the LLM treated `current_stock: 0`
   conservatively and declined the out-of-stock item while fulfilling the in-stock items.


4. SUGGESTIONS FOR FURTHER IMPROVEMENTS
---------------------------------------

Suggestion 1: Margin-Aware Dynamic Pricing & Wholesale/Retail Price Separation
- Separate wholesale supplier cost (e.g., 70% of retail list price) from retail `unit_price`,
  or make `calculate_bulk_quote` margin-aware by applying full bulk discounts (5%-15%) only
  to quantities fulfilled from existing warehouse inventory while capping discounts on
  supplier-restocked quantities so every sale guarantees a positive net cash margin.

Suggestion 2: Unit-of-Measure Normalization & Semantic Search (RAG) Over Quote History
- Add a deterministic unit normalizer inside the inventory and quoting tools to convert
  "reams" (500 sheets), "packets" (25 envelopes), and "boxes" into canonical base units
  before stock and price calculations.
- Enhance `search_quote_history` with TF-IDF / vector similarity search (as in Lesson 7)
  so historical quotes are matched by semantic similarity across job types and event sizes.

Suggestion 3: Structured Inter-Agent Schemas & Dedicated QA / Communication Agent (5th Agent)
- Use the 5th allowed agent slot for a `QualityAssuranceAgent` (and enforce Pydantic output
  schemas between worker agents) that validates outgoing customer responses—ensuring every
  requested item is accounted for (either fulfilled or explicitly rejected) and verifying
  arithmetic totals.
